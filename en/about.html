Our rules

  • We always name the original source.
  • We show when the data was retrieved and what period it covers.
  • We update month-end values once a month, at the start of the month.
  • When we process data (for example, picking month-end values from daily data), we explain how.
  • We never fill in unpublished values by guessing.
  • Stock index values are copyrighted by the companies that calculate them, so we link to the official sites instead of copying them.
  • When a source revises past values, we reflect the change at our next update.

Disclaimer
