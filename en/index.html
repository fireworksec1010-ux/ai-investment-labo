Exchange rates and interest rates now

This month: what happens when the yen gets weaker?

  • Travel abroad: buying the same $1 item costs more yen.
  • Imported goods: things Japan buys from overseas, like wheat and energy, tend to get more expensive.
  • Foreign stocks and funds: even if the dollar price stays the same, the value in yen gets bigger (the opposite happens when the yen gets stronger).

Economics in 30 seconds

Why do stock prices go up and down?

A stock price is set by the balance between people who want to buy and people who want to sell. When more people think a company will make more money in the future, more people want to buy, and the price tends to rise.

But a price can fall even when a company does well. If everyone already expected the good news, it may already be "priced in." What is a stock?
